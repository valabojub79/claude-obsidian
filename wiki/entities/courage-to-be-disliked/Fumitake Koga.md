# Fumitake Koga

Award-winning Japanese professional writer. Co-authored [[courage-to-be-disliked]] with [[Ichiro Kishimi]]. Koga encountered [[Adlerian Psychology]] in his late twenties and found it deeply resonant. He sought out Kishimi to collaborate on a book that would make Adlerian ideas accessible to a general audience. Koga's contribution was the dialogue format and the narrative voice of the skeptical Youth — structured to mirror the reader's natural objections.

## Role in The Courage to be Disliked

Koga authored the book's accessible, confrontational prose style. The Youth character represents the reader's skeptical inner voice. His background in business-related non-fiction writing gives the book its clarity and forward momentum.
