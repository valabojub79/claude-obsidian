# Ichiro Kishimi

Japanese philosopher and [[Adlerian Psychology|Adlerian]] psychologist. He lectures on Adlerian psychology across Europe and Japan, provides certified counseling, and serves as a consultant for the Japanese Society of Adlerian Psychology. He has translated [[Alfred Adler]]'s key works into Japanese, including *The Science of Living*, *Problems of Neurosis*, and *Introduction to Adlerian Psychology*.

## Role in The Courage to be Disliked

Co-authored [[courage-to-be-disliked]] with [[Fumitake Koga]]. Kishimi provided the Adlerian philosophical depth; Koga shaped it into the accessible dialogue format. The Philosopher character in the book is understood to represent Kishimi's voice.
