# Robert K. Greenleaf

American management researcher who coined and popularized the concept of servant leadership, drawing on a long career at AT&T before founding what became the Greenleaf Center for Servant Leadership.

## Contribution

Greenleaf's essay "The Servant as Leader" (1970) introduced [[Servant Leadership]]: the idea that the leader's primary role is to serve the needs of the team first, with formal authority following from that service rather than preceding it.

## Significance

Listed by [[leadership-styles-muse]] among the researchers grounding its leadership-style catalog, alongside [[Kurt Lewin]] and [[Bernard M. Bass]]. The multidimensional servant-leadership models cited by [[leadership-styles-managing-life-at-work]] (Liden et al. 2008/2015) operationalize Greenleaf's original concept.
