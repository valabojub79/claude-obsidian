# Yoshua Bengio

Head of the **LISA lab** (now **Mila**), whose [[lisa-deep-learning-tutorials|Deep Learning Tutorials]] and [[Theano]] library taught a generation. Turing Award 2018 (with Hinton and LeCun).

## Where his work appears in this wiki

- **Unsupervised pretraining**: stacked autoencoders (Bengio 2007) and the monograph *Learning Deep Architectures for AI* (2009). See [[Unsupervised Pretraining]].
- **Attention for NMT**: Bahdanau, Cho, **Bengio** 2014 ([[Neural Machine Translation by Jointly Learning to Align and Translate]]).
- Neural language model (2003), discussed in [[Word Embeddings]]; Glorot and Bengio initialization ([[Weight Initialization]]).
- Paper notes co-authored: [[Multi-Way, Multilingual Neural Machine Translation with a Shared Attention Mechanism]], [[A Character-Level Decoder without Explicit Segmentation for Neural Machine Translation]], [[Hierarchical Multiscale Recurrent Neural Networks]], [[Professor Forcing - A New Algorithm for Training Recurrent Networks]], [[A Structured Self-Attentive Sentence Embedding]].
- Long-term dependency difficulties (Bengio, Simard, Frasconi 1994), cited in [[LISA Tutorial - LSTM Sentiment Analysis]].
