# Andrew Ng

Teacher of the five-course **Deep Learning Specialization** summarized in [[deeplearning-ai-summary|DeepLearning.ai Summary]]. He co-founded Coursera and Google Brain, and was chief scientist at Baidu.

## Signature ideas in the wiki

- Deep learning took off because of **data scale, compute and algorithms** ([[DLAI Course 1 - Neural Networks and Deep Learning]]).
- The **bias/variance recipe**, [[Orthogonalization]], **satisficing vs optimizing metrics**, and **avoidable bias** relative to [[Human-Level Performance]] ([[DLAI Course 3 - Structuring Machine Learning Projects]], which draws on his book *Machine Learning Yearning*).
- Practical defaults: He init, Adam, BatchNorm, random rather than grid search, and "pandas vs caviar" tuning ([[Hyperparameter Tuning]]).
- Prefers reading equations over LSTM diagrams; prefers single-pass detectors (YOLO) over region-proposal pipelines.
- Co-author of the IMDB word-vector paper (Maas et al. 2011) used in [[LISA Tutorial - LSTM Sentiment Analysis]].
