# Denny Britz

Author of the [[deeplearning-papernotes|Deep Learning Paper Notes]] repository (99 detailed notes plus a 627-paper reading list, 2015-2018) and of the WildML blog, known for its RNN and TensorFlow tutorials. Focus: NLP, sequence models and deep RL. He co-authored "Massive Exploration of Neural Machine Translation Architectures" (2017, in the reading list).

His notes stand out for **candid critique**: fairness of comparisons, missing ablations, unreported variance and training time, and whether added complexity beats tuned simple baselines. The hub page summarizes these recurring critiques as a reusable checklist.
