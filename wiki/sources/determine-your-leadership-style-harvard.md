# How to Determine What My Leadership Style Is

Career-development blog post by **Lian Parsons** for **Harvard's Division of Continuing Education**. Practical, self-assessment focused; no named academic frameworks cited.

## What this source adds

The strongest statement of the batch's recurring thesis that **leadership style is not predetermined**: "we are always growing and changing." Supplies the practical how-to for diagnosing your own style.

Names eight styles: autocratic, bureaucratic, coaching, democratic, laissez-faire, pacesetter, servant, visionary. Introduces **pacesetter** (lead by example, results-driven) and **visionary** (long-term goal orientation), which the other fetched sources fold into authoritative or transformational. See [[Leadership Style]] for the catalog.

## How to determine your style (the article's payload)

Self-reflect on priorities (goals vs relationships, structure vs creativity); test styles over short periods; seek mentorship; request team feedback; take a leadership-style assessment.
