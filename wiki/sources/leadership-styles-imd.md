# The 6 Most Common Leadership Styles & How to Find Yours

Business-school blog post from **IMD**, a top executive-education institution. Author not credited.

## What this source adds

Introduces **leadership agility** as the master competency: the capacity to shift approaches based on situational needs while staying authentic to core values. Frames self-awareness (via 360-degree reviews) as the route to it. This is the conceptual bridge to [[Situational Leadership]].

Six styles: transformational, delegative (laissez-faire), authoritative, transactional, participative (democratic), servant. Notable for naming **authoritative** ("follow me" visionary-mentor) as distinct from autocratic, and for flagging that participative leadership is hard to run in remote/decentralized teams.

## Thesis

Most leaders adapt their style to the situation; the best develop leadership agility on top of a known natural style.
