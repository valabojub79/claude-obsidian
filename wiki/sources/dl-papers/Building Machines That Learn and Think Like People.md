# Building Machines That Learn and Think Like People

[Paper](http://arxiv.org/abs/1604.00289) (2016) | Notes by Denny Britz | Index: [[deeplearning-papernotes|Deep Learning Paper Notes]]

**TL;DR:** Lake, Ullman, Tenenbaum and Gershman compare deep learning to human learning through two challenges: recognizing new handwritten characters and objects, and learning the Atari game Frostbite.

## Arguments

- Humans have early **intuitive physics and psychology** ("start-up software") that speeds up new learning.
- Humans build **causal models**, not just pattern recognizers. AlphaGo played about a billion games; Lee Sedol perhaps 50,000.
- Key ingredients: **compositionality**, **learning to learn** (transfer), **causality**.
- Humans use both model-free and model-based learning.
