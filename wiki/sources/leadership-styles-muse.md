# 10 Most Common Leadership Styles and Their Pros and Cons

Practitioner-oriented catalog by **Kat Boogaard** for **The Muse**, quoting career coaches Joyel Crawford and Tara Padua. Grounds its taxonomy in researchers [[Kurt Lewin]], [[Bernard M. Bass]], and [[Robert K. Greenleaf]].

## What this source adds

Broadest catalog of the batch: ten named styles, each with one pro, one con, and a "best used when." The only fetched source to treat [[Situational Leadership]] and charismatic leadership as distinct named styles.

The ten: transactional, transformational, servant, democratic, autocratic, bureaucratic, laissez-faire, charismatic, coaching, situational. See [[Leadership Style]] for the consolidated comparison.

## Thesis

No single perfect style exists; effectiveness is contextual and a style can be changed. "Your leadership style isn't an annual membership." Coaching, servant, situational, and transformational styles are increasingly valued.
