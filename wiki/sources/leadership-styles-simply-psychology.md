# Leadership Styles (Simply Psychology)

Psychology-education article by **Charlotte Nickerson**, reviewed by **Saul McLeod, PhD**. The most historically rooted source in the batch.

## What this source adds

The **origin story**: [[Kurt Lewin]]'s 1939 study (*Experiments in social space*, Harvard Educational Review) as the foundation all later taxonomies build on. Establishes [[Lewin's Leadership Styles]] (autocratic, democratic, laissez-faire) as the canonical base three.

Adds **strategic leadership** (links long-term vision to current context) as a distinct style, and gives the [[Coaching Leadership]] page its flagship example: **Satya Nadella** shifting Microsoft's culture from "knowing" to learning (Berg & Karlsen, 2016).

## Key empirical claims

- Kahai et al. (2004): group efficacy was higher under the transactional condition.
- MacKenzie et al. (2001): transformational behaviors have a stronger relationship with sales performance than transactional, contrary to common sales-industry practice.
