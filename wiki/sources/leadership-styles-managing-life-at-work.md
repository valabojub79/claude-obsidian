# Leadership Styles: Definition, Types, and Examples

The most academically grounded source in the batch. Cites primary research throughout (Lewin/Lippitt/White 1939, Fleishman 1953, Bass & Riggio 2006, Brown/Trevino/Harrison 2005, Walumbwa et al. 2008, Liden et al. 2008/2015).

## What this source adds

A **four-category meta-taxonomy** that organizes the otherwise-flat lists from the other sources:

1. Decision-making styles: participative, autocratic, laissez-faire.
2. Everyday styles: consideration, initiating structure, transactional.
3. Beyond-everyday styles: visionary, transformational, empowering.
4. Values-based styles: ethical, authentic, servant.

It supplies the rigorous definitions: transformational's four dimensions (idealized influence, inspirational motivation, intellectual stimulation, individualized consideration) and servant leadership's seven dimensions. This taxonomy structures the [[Leadership Style]] hub page.

## Key empirical claim

"Leadership styles have stronger effects on effectiveness than leaders' traits and skills." [[Servant Leadership]] shows superior outcomes, exceeding transformational and ethical leadership.
