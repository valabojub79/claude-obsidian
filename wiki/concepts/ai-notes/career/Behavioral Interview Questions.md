# Behavioral Interview Questions

A merged page for the 9 short files in [[ai-notes|AI-Notes]]' `Behavioral Questions/` folder (each just a list of prompts). Answer with **STAR**: Situation, Task, Action, Result (quantify the result).

## Self-introduction (structure of the source's sample)

Greeting and thanks, then current degree and focus, then most recent role and its **measurable impact**, then earlier internships or projects (what was built, results), then freelance or other work, then a close tied to the role. (The source's sample is the AI-Notes author's own bio; only the structure is kept here.)

## Teamwork

Helping a teammate succeed; collaborating with someone very different; an interpersonal conflict; a teammate who won't or can't deliver; mentoring or being mentored; disagreeing with a supervisor; feeling part of a team; training new hires.

## Adaptability

Learning the ropes in a first job; long hours; a sudden change at work; extreme stress and how you managed it; performing under pressure; switching between in-office, remote and hybrid work.

## Communication

Persuading a coworker; misunderstanding a task; handling criticism; disagreeing with a performance review; a poor communicator as a manager; explaining tech to non-technical people; views on 1:1s; a successful presentation.

## Leadership

Leading a project; delegating well; a proud achievement; a task with no prior experience; improving company culture.

## Problem-solving

An unknown problem and how you cracked it; solving something outside your role; a domain problem walk-through; productivity improvements.

## Success and failure

Going above and beyond; being congratulated; a missed goal; a risky decision that failed; realizing you were wrong; habits to improve.

## Time management

Tight deadlines; running a long project; an overwhelming to-do list; personal goals; too much work and too little time from a manager.

## Clients and customers

Correcting a client who was wrong; a frustrating stakeholder; a client situation you'd handle differently.
