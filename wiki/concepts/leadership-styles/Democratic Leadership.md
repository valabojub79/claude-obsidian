# Democratic Leadership

Also called **participative** leadership. One of [[Lewin's Leadership Styles]], and the single most-cited style across the batch (all five sources). The leader guides but actively involves the team in decision-making and shares responsibility ([[leadership-styles-simply-psychology]]).

## Pros

- Encourages creativity and innovation; higher-quality contributions.
- Higher morale, cooperation, and ownership; builds trust and commitment.
- Reduces failure risk through diverse perspectives.

## Cons

- Slower decisions; consensus-building can be inefficient and costly.
- Potential over-dependence on team members.
- [[leadership-styles-imd]]: hard to run in remote or decentralized teams, where the required spontaneous, candid communication is difficult.

## Best used when

There is adequate time for decisions, team members have relevant expertise, and organizational buy-in matters ([[leadership-styles-muse]], [[leadership-styles-simply-psychology]]).

## Tactics

[[leadership-styles-managing-life-at-work]] decomposes participation into consultation, joint decision-making, and delegation.
