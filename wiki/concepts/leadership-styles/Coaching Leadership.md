# Coaching Leadership

The leader acts as a coach and mentor, developing each person by recognizing and building on their individual strengths and weaknesses ([[determine-your-leadership-style-harvard]], [[leadership-styles-muse]]).

## Pros

- Builds trust, improving employee satisfaction, motivation, and retention.
- [[leadership-styles-simply-psychology]] (Berg & Karlsen 2016): replaces short-term problem-solving with longer-term strategic thinking.

## Cons

- Time- and effort-intensive, especially for large teams.

## Best used when

Organizations with skepticism toward leadership, or low engagement despite a skilled team ([[leadership-styles-muse]]).

## Flagship example

**Satya Nadella at Microsoft** shifted the culture from "knowing" to valuing learning from mistakes, a frequently cited case of coaching leadership at scale ([[leadership-styles-simply-psychology]]). Overlaps with the individualized-consideration dimension of [[Transformational Leadership]].
