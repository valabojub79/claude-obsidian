# Autocratic Leadership

Also called **authoritarian** leadership. One of [[Lewin's Leadership Styles]]. The leader makes decisions independently and dictates how tasks are done, with a clear hierarchical separation between leader and team ([[leadership-styles-simply-psychology]], [[leadership-styles-muse]]).

## Pros

- Fast, decisive action; eliminates confusion and guesswork.
- Reduces stress in high-pressure situations.
- Works where subordinates lack knowledge of the problem.

## Cons

- Stifles creativity and innovation.
- Employees may feel ignored, restricted, or abused; fear and frustration, reduced communication.
- [[leadership-styles-simply-psychology]] notes transitions into and out of this style are particularly difficult.

## Best used when

Complex crises, urgent decisions, close supervision needed, or rapidly streamlining workflows ([[leadership-styles-muse]], [[leadership-styles-simply-psychology]]).

## Notes

The Muse and IMD use "my way or the highway" for both autocratic and transactional; the distinction is that [[Transactional Leadership]] adds an explicit reward/penalty exchange, whereas autocratic is about unilateral decision authority. [[leadership-styles-imd]] separates a softer **authoritative** ("follow me" mentor) style from autocratic.
