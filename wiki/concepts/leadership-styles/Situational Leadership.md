# Situational Leadership

A flexible meta-style: rather than applying one blanket method, the leader adapts their approach to the specific circumstances, team, and moment ([[leadership-styles-muse]]). It is less a fixed style than the resolution of the whole field's central thesis (see [[Leadership Style]]).

## Pros

- Delivers what a given situation or team needs at a given time.

## Cons

- Demands significant situational awareness and emotional intelligence.

## Best used when

Constantly changing teams, or diverse personalities with different preferences ([[leadership-styles-muse]]).

## Relationship to leadership agility

[[leadership-styles-imd]] frames the same idea as **leadership agility**: shifting approaches based on situational needs while staying authentic to core values. Self-awareness (via 360-degree reviews) is the prerequisite. This is the capability most sources in the batch ultimately recommend building on top of a known natural style.
