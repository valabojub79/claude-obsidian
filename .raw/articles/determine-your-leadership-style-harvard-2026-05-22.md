# How to Determine What My Leadership Style Is

- Author: Lian Parsons, Harvard Division of Continuing Education
- Publication: Harvard Professional & Executive Development blog

## Styles discussed (8)

1. Autocratic - strong focus on input and decision-making from the person in charge; clear hierarchical separation; structured environments.
2. Bureaucratic - characterized by hierarchy, defined duties, chain of command, established rules and processes.
3. Coaching - personalized; improves employees as individuals by focusing on unique strengths and weaknesses.
4. Democratic - shared decision-making among team members; encourages participation and collective talent.
5. Laissez-faire - largely hands-off; employees expected to make their own decisions.
6. Pacesetter - leaders lead by example; value results and take initiative to pursue goals.
7. Servant - prioritizes well-being and harmony among team members through listening, empathy, community building.
8. Visionary - long-term goal for the future, working toward it with others.

## Main argument

Leadership styles are NOT predetermined. "You may lean more toward one style than another. However, we are always growing and changing." Style can be developed.

## How to determine your style

- Self-reflection on priorities (goals vs relationships, structure vs creativity)
- Test styles over short periods
- Seek mentorship
- Request team feedback
- Take leadership style assessments
