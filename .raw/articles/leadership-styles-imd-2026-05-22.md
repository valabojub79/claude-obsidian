# The 6 Most Common Leadership Styles & How to Find Yours

- Publication: IMD (International Institute for Management Development) blog, Leadership category
- Author: not credited

## Main thesis

Most leaders adapt their leadership styles to suit their situation. The most successful develop "leadership agility": the capacity to shift approaches based on situational needs while staying authentic to core values.

## Six styles

1. Transformational - emphasizes change and inspiration; future-focused vision; encourages followers to exceed perceived capabilities. Best for organizations undergoing significant transformation.
2. Delegative (Laissez-Faire) - "one of the least intrusive forms of leadership"; trusts employees to manage their own work. Strength: leverages creativity/autonomy with competent teams. Weakness: division, unclear accountability, hard for newcomers.
3. Authoritative - visionary "follow me" approach emphasizing mentorship and personalized feedback. Risk: micromanagement, scalability.
4. Transactional - structured reward-and-punishment systems. Clear expectations, efficient task completion. Weakness: diminishing returns; requires consistent incentives.
5. Participative (Democratic) - inclusive, involves employees in decisions, shared power. Strength: trust, empowerment. Weakness: hard in remote/decentralized settings.
6. Servant - prioritizes others' needs; ethical decision-making foundation; empowerment-oriented. Strength: creativity, cross-hierarchy communication, loyalty.

## Frameworks/tools

- 360-degree reviews for self-assessment
- "Leadership agility" as the key competency
